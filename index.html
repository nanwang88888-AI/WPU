01 / About

Scholar. Educator. Mentor.

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at William Paterson University of New Jersey. My academic work brings together emerging computing technologies and real-world applications, from generative AI and blockchain to computational biology and data science.

I am actively involved in developing graduate computer science education, guiding undergraduate research, and expanding opportunities for students to engage in hands-on computing projects.
